Porting my Python physics engine to JavaScript showed 
me how important it is to have a modular design. 
Many of the physics calculations and integrator 
methods transferred directly because they were 
already separated into classes and functions. 
However, adding multiple bodies, collision handling, 
and rendering made me realize how much easier development 
is when each component has its own responsibility. 
If I started over, I would organize the Python engine into
 separate modules for bodies, forces, integrators, and collisions from the beginning. 
 I would also keep the physics calculations completely separate from visualization 
 so the engine could be reused and tested more easily.
